# Sources and interpretation

Information and access terms can change; consult the source pages before a proposal or deployment.

## UTOPYS and Radboud

- [UTOPYS overview](https://www.utopys.org/): ten-year Dutch research programme, EMT in real time, model and synthetic data library.
- [UTOPYS research](https://www.utopys.org/research): M1 dynamic stochastic surrogates, M2 stability of self-organising systems, M3 blackout risk, C1 WAMPAC, C2 predictive-control digital twins, C3 information layers, P1 policy/regulation, P2 network growth and P3 markets/stability. Gabriel Lord is listed as a co-lead of Mathematical Principles.
- [UTOPYS facility and access](https://www.utopys.org/facility): RTDS NovaCor, commissioning, gradual co-simulation interface rollout and external applications expected in 2029. Collaboration through a consortium member remains subject to agreement and access arrangements.
- [Radboud UQ research context](https://www.ru.nl/en/working-at/job-opportunities/postdoc-position-uncertainty-quantification-for-power-systems): parameter and topology identification, PMU measurements, model uncertainty and power systems. This is cited to understand research needs; the proposed interaction is **cooperation, not a job application**.
- [Gabriel Lord](https://www.math.ru.nl/~glord/) and [Laura Scarabosio](https://www.math.ru.nl/~scarabosio/): research interests.

The app labels the relationships to UCF as *proposed* contributions. It does not claim that UCF, Wasserstein-DRO, this app or its outputs are operational, implemented or validated within UTOPYS.
