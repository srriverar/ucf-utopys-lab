The expected-value formulation
C0(Ps) = Δt [ co ∫0Pₛ (Ps − p) f0(p) dp  +  cu ∫PₛPmax (p − Ps) f0(p) dp ]

P is available power; Pₛ is the schedule. Penalties are in €/MWh, Δt in hours and power in MW; the result is euros per interval.

Read the model before reading the numbers.

This is a self-contained educational prototype for a possible UTOPYS collaboration—not an official UTOPYS tool, an EMT simulator, a calibrated forecast or a reproduction of the EGA Explorer.

  • Both inputs are synthetic beta distributions on [0, 100] MW, approximated by a 200-point midpoint probability measure. Real wind and solar may have point masses at zero or rated capacity, and spatial and temporal dependence.
  • The robust curve is the dual solution for that discretised reference, continuous bounded support and the specified linear penalties. An operational study requires data-driven costs, a defensible radius, network constraints and out-of-sample validation.
  • A scalar UCF does not predict converter dynamics, guarantee stability or replace EMT simulations. Sensitivity bounds for discontinuous failure indicators require additional analysis.
